# WillCrafty Pricing

> The basic WillCrafty will draft remains free. Paid options focus on execution support, review coordination, family workflows, and agent licensing.

## Free Draft

- Guided browser-only will draft.
- PDF, Word-compatible HTML, and plain text exports.
- Jurisdiction disclaimer and signing reminders.
- No account required.
- No server-side will draft storage in the static app.

## Paid Options

- **WillCrafty Plus:** Execution pack for users who want a signing checklist, storage checklist, and beneficiary notification support.
- **Human Review Coordination:** Paid path to coordinate review with a qualified professional where available.
- **Family Planning Workflow:** Support for couples or families who need a more complete planning flow.
- **Agent Licensing:** A B2B2C version for insurance agents, financial planners, and estate-planning partners.

## Positioning

WillCrafty competes on clarity, privacy, speed, and execution support rather than pretending that a downloaded draft alone solves every legal requirement.
