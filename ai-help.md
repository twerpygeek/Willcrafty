# WillCrafty AI Help

> WillCrafty includes an AI helper for general self-help explanations about the will drafting process.

## What The AI Helper Can Explain

- What to prepare before drafting a will.
- What an executor usually does.
- Why beneficiaries should be named clearly.
- Why witnesses should not be beneficiaries.
- Why the downloaded draft should be printed, signed, witnessed, and stored safely.
- How beneficiary notification drafts work.

## Privacy Boundary

The AI helper only receives the question typed into the helper box. Users should avoid pasting sensitive will details unless they intentionally want those details included in the AI request.

## Legal Boundary

AI responses are general self-help information and are not legal advice. Users should review local requirements and consult a qualified professional before signing.
