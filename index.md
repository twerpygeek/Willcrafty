# WillCrafty

> Your will, your way: quick, clear, and completely free.

WillCrafty helps people create a private will draft in a guided browser-only flow. The basic draft is free and can be downloaded as PDF, Word-compatible HTML, or plain text.

## Why WillCrafty Exists

Estate planning often fails at the practical steps: people delay, forget who needs to know, misunderstand signing rules, or never store the original safely. WillCrafty turns the first draft into a simple sequence and reminds users that proper execution still matters.

## Core Promise

- No hidden fees for the basic draft.
- No account required.
- No server-side will draft storage in the static app.
- Clear export options.
- Plain-language signing reminders.

## How It Works

1. Answer guided questions about identity, jurisdiction, executor, beneficiaries, assets, and wishes.
2. Review the generated draft and jurisdiction disclaimer.
3. Download the will draft as PDF, Word-compatible HTML, or plain text.
4. Print and sign according to local requirements.
5. Store the signed original safely and prepare notification emails.
