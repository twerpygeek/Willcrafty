# Create A Will With WillCrafty

> WillCrafty provides a guided, browser-only workspace for creating a private will draft.

## What Users Prepare

- Full legal name and basic identity details.
- Signing jurisdiction or location.
- Executor name and contact details.
- Beneficiaries and their intended shares.
- Assets, property, accounts, or personal items to distribute.
- Personal wishes, funeral preferences, guardianship notes, or special instructions.

## Drafting Steps

1. Personal information.
2. Executor appointment.
3. Beneficiaries.
4. Asset distributions.
5. Personal wishes.
6. Review and download.

## Privacy Model

The static WillCrafty builder keeps the draft in the browser during the session. The app does not require an account and does not store the completed will on WillCrafty servers.

## Legal Reminder

WillCrafty is a self-help drafting tool. A will generally needs to be printed, signed voluntarily by a person of sound mind, witnessed by competent adult witnesses who are not beneficiaries, and reviewed against local law. Requirements vary by jurisdiction.
