Brand guidelines

Clear, calm, crafted.

WillCrafty should feel like a trustworthy product that respects both the gravity of estate planning and the user’s desire to finish without friction.

Logo

The mark combines a document, checkmark, and folded corner. Use it with generous whitespace and keep it in Tiffany Blue whenever possible.

Typography

Use a modern system sans stack. Headlines are large, direct, and tightly spaced. Body copy stays plainspoken, readable, and never legalistic.

Your will, your way. Quick, clear, and completely free.

Motion

Motion should feel cinematic but quiet: staggered reveals, floating document cards, progress movement, and short spring feedback. Avoid gimmicks.

  • Reveal sections upward over 650ms.
  • Use slow looping motion only on decorative previews.
  • Keep form transitions under 350ms.
  • Honor readable, calm pacing for a legal-adjacent task.
